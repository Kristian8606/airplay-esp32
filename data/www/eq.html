Audio EQ

Independent biquad chains for the left and right physical outputs.

Source & Headroom

Each physical output always has its own independent filters. In Mono, Left and Right receive the same mono source but are processed separately, so one output can be low-pass and the other high-pass.

Right Output Filters

Up to 24 filters per output. PK uses Frequency/Gain/Q. LP, HP, BP and Notch use Frequency/Q. LS and HS use Frequency/Gain.

Apply

Settings are not changed live. Save writes the complete configuration to NVS, then the ESP restarts and builds both filter chains from clean state.
