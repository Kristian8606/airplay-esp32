Audio EQ

Independent biquad chains for the left and right physical outputs.

Mute buttons act immediately, even with EQ disabled. Outputs are unmuted after restart.

Source

Each physical output always has its own independent filters. In Mono, Left and Right receive the same mono source but are processed separately, so one output can be low-pass and the other high-pass.

Loudness

Keeps the sound full when you turn it down. At low volume the ear hears less bass and a little less treble; this puts them back, the more the quieter you listen, following the ISO 226 equal-loudness standard. Works with or without the EQ and follows the volume instantly.

Play music at the level you usually call loud and press Use current volume. There is no correction at or above that level; below it the correction grows.

Apply & Save

Applies the EQ live and saves the complete configuration to NVS. No restart is required. Because EQ remains before the PCM buffer, the audible change follows the already-decoded ~1.1 s PCM lead.
